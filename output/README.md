# Shared results (aggregated, non-personal)

The mobile phone data used in the paper were supplied by Locomizer under licence and cannot be
redistributed. Neither the raw location pings and stays nor any per-user intermediate result
(home and work locations, visited hexagons, weekly CO₂e per user) is included in this repository.

This folder contains the aggregated tables from which the figures of the paper and its
Supplementary Material are drawn. They were produced from the per-user results with
`tools/make_shared_outputs.py`. Two rules protect individual users:

- **No individual records.** Distributions are released as histogram counts (0.1 kg CO₂e bins,
  0.05 kg for commuting) and summary statistics; every row describes a group of users.
- **Minimum group size for spatial aggregates.** Hexagon (H3 resolution 9) and grid values are
  released only where at least **5 users** contribute (`K_MIN` in the script). The 1 km grid
  comparison is released without grid identifiers.

Weekly CO₂e values are in kilograms unless stated otherwise. `mode` takes the values `bike`,
`pt`, `car` and, where relevant, `car_bev` (car results rescaled by the BEV emission factor,
70/162 gCO₂e/km, which is how the electrification scenario in Fig. 4 is obtained).

| File | Content | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| `{pt,bike,car}_map_data_overall{,_turku,_tampere,_oulu}.parquet` | Per origin hexagon (`from_id`): number and share of POIs of each category, and of jobs, reachable within a 1 kg CO₂e round trip by the given mode. Derived from routing and POI data only (no mobile phone data). Helsinki files carry no suffix. | Fig. 1 |
| `decent_mobility_weekly_co2_distribution.csv` | Histogram of weekly decent-mobility CO₂e per user: `city` (four cities and `Overall`), `mode`, `bin_lower_kg`, `bin_upper_kg`, `n_users`. Values above 60 kg are pooled in one bin. | Fig. 2a, Fig. 4a, Suppl. Fig. S1 |
| `decent_mobility_weekly_co2_summary.csv` | Per city and mode: number of users, mean, standard deviation, percentiles (5, 10, 25, 50, 75, 90, 95), box-plot whiskers (1.5 × IQR) and the share of users within 3.8, 5.6, 7.0 and 8.4 kg CO₂e per week. | Fig. 2a-b, Fig. 4b, Suppl. Figs. S1-S2 |
| `decent_mobility_home_hexagons.parquet` | Per city, mode and home hexagon (`home_gid9`): `n_users`, `mean_co2_kg`, `median_co2_kg`, and `in_city_filter` (hexagon lies inside the city extent used in the maps). Hexagons with fewer than 5 users withheld. | Fig. 2c, Suppl. Figs. S3-S6 |
| `fig3_compliance_by_income_decile.csv` | Per city, mode and within-city income decile: `n_users`, `compliance_rate` (share ≤ 7 kg), `avg_co2_kg`, `median_co2_kg` and the decile's income range (median annual income of the 250 m grid cell of residence, Statistics Finland 2021). | Fig. 3a |
| `fig3_income_pt_co2_hexagons.parquet` | Per city and home hexagon: `n_users`, `mean_income_eur`, `mean_pt_co2_kg`, `income_tercile`, `co2_tercile`. Terciles computed over all hexagons of the city before withholding hexagons with fewer than 5 users. | Fig. 3b |
| `home_work_hourly_presence.csv` | Share of stays at the inferred home, work or other location by hour of day and day type (`work_day` Mon-Fri, `non_work_day`), with the underlying stay counts. | Suppl. Fig. S7 |
| `population_vs_users_1km.csv`, `population_vs_users_1km_totals.json` | Census population (Statistics Finland 2024, 1 km grid) and number of sample users with home location in the cell, for cells with users; rows shuffled and without grid identifiers. Totals for the penetration-rate calculation in the JSON file. | Suppl. Fig. S8 |
| `commuting_co2_distribution.csv`, `commuting_co2_summary.csv` | Histogram and summary statistics of the round-trip CO₂e of the observed home-to-work tour per user, by city and mode (after the same minimum-value cleaning as in the figure). | Suppl. Fig. S9b |
| `commuting_home_work_hexagons.parquet` | Number of users per home hexagon and per work hexagon (`location` = `home`/`work`), by city; hexagons with fewer than 5 users withheld. | Suppl. Fig. S9a |
| `exposure_level_sensitivity_{oulu,turku}.csv` | Median and mean weekly CO₂e and share of users within budget as a function of the POI exposure level (0.0 to 1.0), by mode. Only the Oulu and Turku tables were retained from the original runs; the Helsinki and Tampere tables are re-generated by `07_supplementary/D_exposure_level_sensitivity_*.ipynb`. | Suppl. Fig. S10 |

Not included: the two individual cumulative exposure curves of Suppl. Fig. S11 (they are
per-user curves and are shown only as figures in the paper).
